    Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

    Who can sign up for WCC?World Changers Conference is open to everyone—from working professionals and business owners to aspiring entrepreneurs, leaders, and students preparing for the workforce.
    Can participants select more than two workshops?No. If more than two are selected, we will randomly assign two from your selections.
    Is WCC suitable for students?Yes. While the conference is designed for professionals, students will gain valuable insights, practical skills, and perspectives to help them transition confidently into the workplace.
    Are meals provided?Yes. Registration fee is inclusive of lunch and tea breaks.
    How do I know if my registration is confirmed?Your registration is confirmed once payment is received and verified. You will also be notified via email. Unpaid registrations may be forfeited. Please refer to payment details upon registration.
    What does “Professional Networking Session” mean?Connect with delegates in your chosen industry through facilitated networking sessions. Exchange ideas, share experiences, and build meaningful professional connections.
    Must the delegate choose the industry of their occupation for the networking session?No. You're welcome to join any industry group that aligns with your interests or career aspirations.
    What happens if I can no longer attend the event?Registration fees are non-refundable. However, you may transfer your registration to another participant. Please email us with the replacement participant’s details for assistance.
